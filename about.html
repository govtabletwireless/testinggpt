About FreeGovPhone

FreeGovPhone was founded with the mission of connecting every American with access to modern communication tools. We partner with government programs to ensure eligible individuals receive a free iPhone to stay connected with loved ones, employers, and essential services.

Our dedicated team works tirelessly to simplify the application process and provide exceptional customer support.

Our Team

We are a group of professionals passionate about bridging the digital divide. Our backgrounds range from telecommunications to social services, and we leverage our expertise to deliver outstanding results.
